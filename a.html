Ants and Ladybugs

螞蟻與瓢蟲學習頁

螞蟻介紹

Ant Q&A 1

Describe the ant.

描述這隻螞蟻。

This is an ant.

這是一隻螞蟻。

Ant Q&A 2

What body part does it have?

它有什麼身體部位？

It has six legs and antennae.

牠有六隻腳和觸角。

Ants have six legs and antennae.

螞蟻有六隻腳和觸角。

It has a head, a thorax, and an abdomen.

牠有頭、胸部和腹部。

Ant Q&A 3

Where does it live?

它住在哪裡？

It lives in an anthill.

牠住在蟻丘裡。

Ants live in anthills.

螞蟻住在蟻丘裡。

It lives under the ground.

牠住在地底下。

瓢蟲介紹

Ladybug Q&A 1

Describe the _____. (critter/bug)

描述這個_____。（小動物／昆蟲）

This is a red ladybug. It is tiny.

這是一隻紅色的瓢蟲。牠很小。

Ladybug Q&A 2

What body part does it have?

牠有什麼身體部位？

It has spots and wings.

牠有斑點和翅膀。

Ladybug Q&A 3

What body part does it have?

牠有什麼身體部位？

Ladybugs have spots and wings.

瓢蟲有斑點和翅膀。

Ladybug Q&A 4

What is the life cycle of a ladybug?

瓢蟲的生命週期是什麼？

eggs → larva → pupa → adult

卵 → 幼蟲 → 蛹 → 成蟲

相同地方

Same Q&A

What is the same about the two bugs?

這兩種昆蟲有什麼相同的地方？

Ants and ladybugs both have antennae and head.

螞蟻和瓢蟲都有觸角和頭。

They are both tiny bugs.

牠們都是小昆蟲。

不同地方

Different Q&A

What is the difference between them?

牠們有什麼不同的地方？

Ladybugs have two wings to fly but ants don’t.

瓢蟲有兩片翅膀可以飛，但是螞蟻沒有。
